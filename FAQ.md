# Frequently Asked Questions

---

### What is Childhood Emotional Neglect (CEN)?

CEN describes growing up in a household where your emotional experiences were consistently overlooked, minimized, or left unacknowledged — not necessarily through deliberate cruelty, but through a persistent absence of emotional attunement.

It often shows up in adulthood as: not knowing what you feel, feeling like a burden for having needs, a persistent emptiness, difficulty in relationships, or a sense that something is "off" but you can't name it.

Dr. Jonice Webb coined the term and has written extensively about it. Her books *Running on Empty* and *Running on Empty No More* are the foundational reading.

---

### Is Nureli therapy?

No. Nureli is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical treatment. We're very clear about this — both inside the app and publicly.

If you're in crisis or dealing with trauma that requires clinical support, please reach out to a licensed therapist. If you're in India: [iCall](https://icallhelpline.org) (+91 9152987821). If you're in the US: [Psychology Today's therapist finder](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/therapists).

---

### Can I use Nureli alongside therapy?

Yes — many users do. Some share their reflections with their therapist as a way of surfacing patterns between sessions. A partner mode to facilitate this is on the roadmap.

---

### What does "no streaks" mean in practice?

It means the app never tracks how many days in a row you've used it, never sends you a "you broke your streak" notification, and never makes you feel behind.

You can disappear for three months and come back, and Nureli will meet you where you are — not where it thinks you should be.

---

### Is my data private?

Yes. Your reflections are encrypted and private. We don't sell data. We don't use third-party ad tracking. You can export or delete everything from Settings at any time.

The AI companion features are opt-in and clearly labeled. On-device processing is used wherever possible; when a request goes to the cloud, you'll know.

---

### Why is there no Android version?

We're a small team and we wanted to get the iOS experience right before expanding. Android is on the roadmap — no timeline yet.

---

### How is this different from journaling apps?

Journaling apps give you a blank page. Nureli gives you a prompt, a frame, and a gentle guide for going deeper than you might go on your own.

The difference is the *structure* — each Mirror card is designed around a specific CEN-related pattern (avoidance, inner-child work, self-compassion, etc.), not just a generic "write about your day."

---

### How is this different from therapy apps like BetterHelp?

BetterHelp and similar apps connect you with a licensed therapist for actual therapy sessions. Nureli doesn't do that — we're not a marketplace for therapists and we don't deliver clinical treatment.

We're closer to a thoughtful workbook than a therapy platform. The two can work alongside each other well.
