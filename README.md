## What is Nureli?

Nureli is an iOS app for adults navigating **Childhood Emotional Neglect (CEN)** — the invisible wound that comes from growing up in a home where your emotions were overlooked, minimized, or simply not acknowledged.

It isn't therapy. It isn't a mood tracker. It's a private, unhurried space to:

- Learn what's actually happening inside you
- Practice sitting with feelings without needing to fix them
- Build a relationship with yourself, one small reflection at a time

No streaks. No scores. No pressure. Just presence.

<br/>

## What's Inside

Nureli is organized around four spaces, each with a distinct purpose in the emotional growth journey.

<br/>

### 🏡 Hearth — *Showing up, not tracking*

Your daily home base. Hearth asks one small question: *how are you right now?*

- Check in with words — or without them (for days when language feels inaccessible)
- A gentle weekly reflection surfaces what you've actually been feeling
- Growth is shown as a bloom — not a streak counter

<br/>

### 🪞 Mirror — *Eleven ways to look inward*

Structured reflection exercises, each targeting a different layer of the inner world.

| Exercise | What it does |
|----------|--------------|
| **Descent** | Guided deep-dive into a feeling you've been avoiding |
| **Witness Stand** | Observe yourself without judgment — just facts |
| **Complaint Box** | Full, guilt-free permission to be frustrated |
| **Inner Child** | Reparenting moments from your past |
| **Unsent Letter** | Closure that doesn't require anyone else |
| **CEN Moments** | Recognize patterns before you repeat them |
| **Daily Ritual** | Ground yourself in the present |
| *+ 4 more* | Unlocked as you move through your journey |

<br/>

### 🌱 Grove — *A 90-day curriculum*

A psychologist-approved learning arc covering CEN from roots to recovery.

- Day 1 → 90, structured and sequenced with intention
- Each lesson is a complete session — not a bullet point
- Scenario-based practice so learning becomes embodied
- **No timers. No progress bars. No Day-N-of-90 labels.** You go at your pace.

<br/>

### 🗺️ Journey — *Your story over time*

Not a dashboard. Not metrics. A real reflection of how far you've come.

- **Then & Now** — compare where you started to where you are (without fabricating data)
- **Letter to Who You Were** — milestone letters from your current self to your past
- **Your Story So Far** — a narrative summary of your growth in your own emotional language
- Emotion constellation built from your actual check-ins

## Philosophy

> *Most apps reward engagement. Nureli rewards honesty.*

We built Nureli because CEN recovery is fundamentally private, non-linear, and impossible to gamify. The app deliberately has no streaks, no leaderboards, no "you're on a roll!" notifications.

We believe:
- **Feelings don't need to be fixed** — they need to be witnessed
- **Progress isn't linear** — and shouldn't look like it is
- **You already know what's inside you** — we're just helping you listen

All curriculum content is based on the clinical framework around CEN developed by Dr. Jonice Webb, reviewed by licensed psychologists.
