## Coming Soon

### Companion Chat (Mirror)
An AI that responds in the context of your actual reflections — not a generic chatbot, but something that remembers what you wrote last week and meets you where you are. Currently in internal testing.

### Android
We're iOS-first while we get the experience right. Android is on the roadmap but we don't have a timeline yet.

### Partner / Relationship Mode
A way to share selective insights with a trusted person — a therapist, a partner, a friend — without opening your whole journal.

---

## In Progress

### Grove: Days 46–90
Deep-session content for the second half of the curriculum. Days 1–45 are fully authored; we're extending one region at a time to maintain clinical quality.

### Content Library Sync
Ability to push curriculum updates without an App Store release, so corrections and new content reach you faster.

## Recently Shipped

### v1.1 — The Reinvention
A full rework of all four tabs. Highlights:
- **Hearth**: The Noticing check-in, No-Words Welcome path, weekly witnessing (receipt-backed, count-free)
- **Mirror**: All 11 cards rebuilt from scratch with shared Pond infrastructure
- **Grove**: Daily lesson flow, Day 1→90 tap-to-begin, measurement cues removed entirely
- **Journey**: Then & Now (regression-aware), Letter to Who You Were, Seasonal Ceremony, Your Story So Far

### v1.0 — Launch
Initial release with core check-in, Mirror (3 cards), and Grove (Days 1–30).

---

## Not Planned

To be clear about what Nureli will never be:

- **Streaks or daily reminders that create guilt** — the pressure to maintain a streak is exactly what CEN survivors don't need
- **Social / public sharing** — your inner world is not content
- **A substitute for therapy** — we'll always say this plainly and link to real support
- **Gamification of any kind** — points, badges, leaderboards: none
